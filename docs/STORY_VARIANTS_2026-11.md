### Why V1 was chosen

- **Who and why within 10 s.** A real founder of a real Latin American company names the audience by
  0:11, with no dramatization tag. This answers W4 in the judge panel ("hides the real
  business") and counts for Impact.
- **One question carries the whole video.** "Can it touch my money?" is asked at 0:15, answered at
  0:30 ("It can hold it. Only you can move it.") and repeated on the closing card. All three
  selectors called it the easiest line to remember.
- **A new threat model.** "An assistant too kind to say no" replaces the generic hacker story,
  which helps against W3 (pattern-matching to generic human-in-the-loop). It is not anti-AI,
  because the goodwill beat ends with the owner saying yes.
- **The full PayPal lifecycle on screen.** Orders v2 AUTHORIZE, signed webhook, capture, void (not
  refund) and refund, with mandate jti = PayPal-Request-Id. Keep, release and give back map onto
  capture, void and refund.
- **An honest closing number from the ledger**: "her whole day of money: three taps, $0.00
  moved by the AI on its own."

Weaknesses the selectors flagged in V1, which the grafts fix:

- The cold-open card is static.
- PayPal is not named until about 0:30.
- 25 s of text cards before product footage (0:15-0:40).
- "Even a retry can't charge twice" is only said, never shown.
- The goodwill beat depends on a nondeterministic live model.
- About 15 h of scope.

> Innovation: the current docs pitch a secure deposit flow.
